## 1. "Flow Funding" — the namesake (Marion Rockefeller Weber)

**VERIFIED.** "Flow Funding" is a named practice coined by **Marion Rockefeller
Weber**, who started the **Flow Fund Circle in May 1991**. An *Initiator* gives
trusted individuals ("Flow Funders") a sum to **give away spontaneously** to
whoever they see need — no applications, no paperwork. Funders may not fund
themselves or relatives; they meet yearly "in circle." The model is recursive:
former Flow Funders become "Circle Holders" who choose their own Flow Funders —
a **layered cascade of giving**.

The load-bearing metaphor (near-verbatim): flow funding is **bio-mimicry of the
circulatory system** — nutrients reach every cell and the blood returns to the
heart; **"the people who are carrying the money are nurtured as well as the ones
who are receiving it."** The carrier is nourished, not just the recipient.

**Maps to brief:** the direct namesake. Contributes: **trust as the routing
mechanism** (fund people, not proposals), **pass-along over accumulation**, **the
carrier is nourished** (relationship-building), **recursive delegation**. What it
deliberately lacks: thresholds + automation (Weber's is intentionally human and
intuitive — our item adds the automation while trying to keep the ethic).

Sources: [flowfunding.org — What is it](https://www.flowfunding.org/about/whatisit.html) ·
[Circle structure](https://www.flowfunding.org/circle/structure.html) ·
[Alliance interview](https://www.alliancemagazine.org/interview/interview-marion-rockefeller-weber/) ·

## 2. The Holomovement — Bohm's physics + the modern movement

**VERIFIED (Bohm).** Physicist **David Bohm** (*Wholeness and the Implicate
Order*, 1980) coined **"holomovement"**: reality is an *"undivided wholeness in
flowing movement"* where **flow is prior to the "things"** that form and dissolve
in it. Things are *relatively stable vortices in a continuous stream*. The
**implicate order** enfolds everything into everything; the **explicate order**
is what unfolds into appearance. Governing law is **holonomy — the law of the
whole**, not of parts.

**VERIFIED (movement).** *The Holomovement: Embracing Our Collective Purpose to
Unite Humanity* (2023), ed. **Emanuel Kuntzelman & Jill Robinson**, draws on Bohm
to frame a social movement — "wholeness in motion and compassion in action…for
the betterment of all." 🟡 UNVERIFIED: could not confirm Deepak Chopra's specific
contribution from sources.

**Maps to brief (mostly INFERENCE):** "flow as primary, things as secondary"
reframes the economics so **the flow of value is the real entity and balances are
temporary vortices**. A pile that sits still is a vortex that *stopped
participating in the whole* — which is the philosophical justification for
**demurrage** and **overflow**. "Building strong relationships" ≈ **holonomy**: a
node's viability is governed by the health of the whole network, not its private
balance.

Sources: [Holomovement — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomovement) ·
[Implicate/explicate order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Implicate_and_explicate_order) ·
[SAND — Bohm](https://scienceandnonduality.com/article/david-bohm-implicate-order-and-holomovement/) ·

## 4. Demurrage & anti-hoarding currency

**VERIFIED.** **Silvio Gesell** proposed **Freigeld ("free money")** — currency
that **loses value over time** via a stamp tax (demurrage), removing money's
unnatural advantage over goods that rot/cost storage, **decoupling
store-of-value from medium-of-exchange** and forcing circulation. Keynes: "the
idea behind stamped money is sound."

**Wörgl (1932–33):** Austrian town; scrip with **1%/month demurrage** (levy → a
poor fund) **circulated ~14× faster** than the national schilling; unemployment
fell; public works built mid-Depression. Shut down by the Austrian National Bank
(1933).

**Charles Eisenstein, *Sacred Economics*:** builds on Gesell — **negative
interest / decaying currency** because positive interest sets a minimum growth
rate and concentrates wealth; demurrage enables zero-interest lending and a
transition toward gift economy + restoration of the commons.

**Circles UBI:** every person mints their own personal currency with **~7%/year
demurrage** ("a parking fee on money") that, with issuance, **gradually
redistributes from long-holders to newcomers**. Sybil-resisted by a **web of
trust**.

**Maps to brief:** the **anti-hoarding engine** named explicitly. A balance that
sits idle decays → the rational move is to pass it on (= the holomovement
intuition). Circles shows **demurrage + personal currency + trust-edges in one
system** — close to "each node."

Sources: [Demurrage — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demurrage_(currency)) ·
[Sacred Economics ch.12](https://sacred-economics.com/sacred-economics-chapter-12-negative-interest-economics/) ·
[Circles whitepaper](https://handbook.joincircles.net/docs/developers/whitepaper/)

## 5. Streaming / splitting money — Drips, Superfluid, Gitcoin

**VERIFIED — Drips Network is the closest existing mechanism to the brief.** On
Ethereum (Radworks ecosystem). Three primitives: **Streaming** (funds move at a
per-second rate), **Giving** (immediate), **Splitting** (forward a fixed
*fraction* of all incoming funds to a list). The distinctive feature: a project's
split receivers are both **maintainers** (addresses) and **dependencies** (other
projects it relies on). On claiming funds you list the projects you depend on and
forward a % — producing a **global dependency tree** where funds **cascade
arbitrarily deep** from dependents down to dependencies, **permissionlessly**
("funds are never stuck and can always be pushed to keep flowing"). Drip Lists =
up to 200 recipients, settled monthly. Real example: ENS streams to 7 core deps;
funds reach 40+ projects two degrees out.

**VERIFIED — Superfluid:** the general **money-streaming** protocol. **Super
Tokens** flow by the second after one transaction (Constant Flow Agreement), plus
one-to-many **Distributions**. Gas-efficient (only open/close transactions).

**VERIFIED — Gitcoin / Quadratic Funding** (Buterin–Hitzig–Weyl, "Liberal
Radicalism"): a matching pool distributed by a quadratic formula (sum of square
roots, squared) so **breadth of support beats depth of pockets** — anti-whale
fairness weighting. Needs sybil-resistance.

**Maps to brief:** Drips *is* "income flows to dependents and from dependencies
automatically" — **splits + streams + recursive cascade** (minus the *threshold*
trigger and the *pull-when-below-floor* direction, which 1644 adds). Superfluid =
the continuous-stream primitive. QF = the fairness-weighting primitive.

Sources: [Drips](https://www.drips.network/) ·
[Drips docs](https://docs.drips.network/) ·
[Dependency funding](https://www.drips.network/solutions/dependency-funding) ·
[Superfluid](https://superfluid.org/) ·
[Gitcoin QF](https://gitcoin.co/mechanisms/quadratic-funding)

## 6. Overflow / threshold / cascade & cybernetic homeostasis

**VERIFIED (partial).** No single canonical "overflow-above / pull-in-below"
model, but the nearest pieces:

- **"Floor not ceiling" giving** — treat a payout rate as a *minimum*, not a cap
  (Nonprofit Quarterly).
- **Econophysics of redistribution vs mutual aid** (Kato & Hiroi, *Entropy*
  2023): the wealthy contribute **surplus stock above a threshold**; finds
  **voluntary mutual aid** beats coercive taxation on inequality and economic
  flow; ties to Graeber's baseline communism.
- **"Waterfilling" overflow** (VCG redistribution): an algorithm that computes an
  *overflow*, caps the highest values, and redistributes surplus downward — a
  concrete **ceiling-with-overflow** mechanism.
- **Regenerative finance "Rerouting the Flow"** (van Peborgh, 2025): nested
  Commons where capital **stabilises the floor first** (fair wages, food
  security), *then* regenerates; value **circulated, not hoarded**.

**Cybernetics — Stafford Beer's Viable System Model (VSM):** each viable system
regulates flows to keep critical variables **within viability bounds** via
**homeostats**; **algedonic signals** (pain/pleasure) jump straight to the top
when a node breaches bounds; **recursion** — viable systems contain viable
systems at every scale (famously Project Cybersyn, Chile 1971–73).

**Maps to brief:** this is the **threshold logic** — overflow above a ceiling,
pull-in below a floor. VSM gives the engineering vocabulary: node income = a
*critical variable*, set **viability bounds (floor/ceiling)**, **homeostats**
route surplus out and support in automatically, **algedonic alarms** when a
dependent falls below floor.

Sources: [Mutual aid econophysics (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9955460/) ·
[Viable System Model — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viable_system_model) ·
[Rerouting the Flow](https://ernestopvanpeborgh.substack.com/p/rerouting-the-flow-structural-foundations)

## 7. Mutual credit & gift networks — trust-edges as channels

**VERIFIED.** **Mutual credit** creates currency *as participants transact*: a
provider gets a positive balance, a recipient a negative one; **the sum of all
balances is always zero**, no central issuer, no interest. Instances: **Sardex**
(Sardinia 2009, B2B, members start at zero with an interest-free credit limit,
modelled on Swiss **WIR**); **LETS** (Linton, 1983); **Trustlines / Ripple /
Stellar** credit-line networks where a **trustline is a directed weighted edge**
(A trusts B up to a limit) and payments **ripple through chains of
friends-of-friends** via max-flow pathfinding (reviving Hawala); **Holochain
mutual credit / Grassroots Economics (Sarafu)**.

**Maps to brief:** the **"strong relationships" + trust-threshold** layer. Trust
limits on edges *are* the owner's "thresholds": a node sets how much it lets flow
to/from each neighbour, and value routes along the **relationship graph**.
Mutual credit makes **the relationship itself the money** — building edges *is*
creating capacity to flow.

Sources: [Mutual Credit — Holochain blog](https://blog.holochain.org/mutual-credit-part-1-a-new-type-of-cryptocurrency-as-old-as-civilisation/) ·
[Sardex — Monneta](https://monneta.org/en/sardex/) ·
[Trustlines FAQ](https://trustlines-network.github.io/faq.html) ·
[ValueFlows / hREA](https://github.com/h-REA/hREA)

## 8. Care / dependents framing

**VERIFIED.** The **care economy** centres on supporting *dependents* — children,
elders, disabled people — and their (often unpaid) caregivers (~2bn people, ~9%
of global GDP). Humans are an **"altricial species"** (born unable to live
independently), so dependency is intrinsic, not exceptional (echoes **Eva
Kittay's dependency theory**). The distributive principle *"from each according to
ability, to each according to need"* is modelled as **Graeber's "baseline
communism"**; network-model studies find gift/concession economies produce more
equal, healthier outcomes **without guaranteeing reciprocity**.

**ValueFlows / REA** (open vocabulary extending Resource-Event-Agent accounting)
is the **accounting substrate** for representing care-flows and dependency-flows
uniformly, with or without money; reference impl **hREA** on Holochain.

**Maps to brief:** reframes "dependents" broadly (children/elders **and**
open-source maintainers as dependents of those who rely on them). "To each
according to need" = the **pull-below-floor** direction; "from each according to
ability" = the **overflow-above-ceiling** direction. Automating the thresholds is
baseline communism as homeostatic flow control — **care as a continuously-settled
network event, not episodic charity.**

Sources: [A Worldview of Care — Daedalus](https://www.amacad.org/daedalus/worldview-care-new-economics) ·
[ValueFlows](https://www.valueflo.ws/)

## Design primitives distillation (for DESIGN)

A flow-funding system composes from a small set of orthogonal primitives:

- **Trust-edges (capacity & limits)** — directed, weighted relationships set the
  channels and caps for flow.
- **Streams** — continuous value movement, not only lump transactions.
- **Splits** — forward a *fraction* of incoming value to a configured list;
  recurse arbitrarily deep.
- **Thresholds: floor & ceiling** — each node's viability band; below floor →
  **pull-in** (need), above ceiling → **overflow-out** (ability).
- **Demurrage** — idle balances decay, forcing circulation + gradual
  redistribution; the anti-hoarding engine.
- **Cascade / overflow routing** — surplus waterfalls down the
  dependency/relationship graph automatically and permissionlessly.
- **Homeostats + algedonic alarms** — automated regulators keep nodes in-band;
  emergency signal when a dependent breaches floor.
- **Fairness weighting** — quadratic/breadth-weighted allocation prevents whale
  capture when many edges compete.
- **Recursion** — the same model at every scale: node, circle, region, whole.
- **Network accounting substrate** — REA/ValueFlows-style events record every
  flow for real-time per-agent and whole-network views.
- **Relationship-as-reward** — the *carrier* of flow is nourished, not just the
  recipient; building edges is the point.

**One-line synthesis (INFERENCE):** 1644 = **Drips-style splits/streams** routed
over **Trustlines-style trust-edges**, bounded by **VSM floor/ceiling
homeostats**, kept circulating by **Gesell/Circles demurrage**, allocated fairly
via **quadratic weighting**, recorded on the **token ledger** — animated by the
**Flow Funding ethic** (pass value along, nourish the carrier) and the
**holomovement worldview** (flow is primary; a balance is just a vortex that must
keep moving).
